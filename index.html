Cognitive Load as a Hidden Cost of Empathy: Tradeoffs in Using VR for Inclusive Design

VR Empathy & Inclusive Design · Human-Computer Interaction · VR Experiment

Xiong, D., Ma, Z., Ling, H., Wu, X., Fan, M., & Zhang, X.

International Journal of Human–Computer Interaction, 2026

We conducted a between-subjects experiment comparing passive video with non-emotional and emotional interactive VR simulations of low-vision mobility challenges. Both VR conditions increased empathy relative to video, but emotional VR produced no additional measurable gain, whereas non-emotional VR produced stronger user-need-aligned ideation. Drawing on cognitive load theory, we argue that emotional intensity can impose a hidden cognitive cost that undercuts the design benefits of empathy.

Recombination and Coordination Processes in Multidisciplinary Academic Entrepreneurial Teams: Explaining Innovative New Venture Idea Generation

Multi-discipline Co-operation · Organization Science · LLM Agent-based Modeling

Opens the process black box between academic entrepreneurial team composition and early-stage new venture idea generation. Using LLM-based agent simulation, it traces how disciplinary differences expand recombination potential while simultaneously creating coordination friction — representing team members with different disciplinary orientations and generating complete interaction traces of how ideas are proposed, challenged, revised and recombined.
